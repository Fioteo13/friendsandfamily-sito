(02) This is us

This is us.

A group of creatives who are passionate about eyewear, united by a shared vision: to design frames we’d wear ourselves and share with our closest circle, crafted with the same care and attention we put into everything we love.

That’s how FRIENDS&FAMILY was born: not just a brand, not just fashion, but a way of life.

Our glasses are like a small “club” born in the city, a shared secret where luxury is never loud, but deeply personal.

A continuous journey fuelled by passion, authenticity and an aesthetic that must first convince us, before it ever reaches the world.
